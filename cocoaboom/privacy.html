Privacy Policy

Cocoa Boom by Chocello Games · Last updated November 18, 2026

This policy describes how Chocello Games (“we,” “us”) handles information in the Cocoa Boom app for Android and iOS (package / Bundle ID com.chocello.cocoaboom). The app is built to work fully offline. We do not run ads, we do not sell in-app purchases in this version, and we do not operate user accounts.

Information we collect

We do not collect personal information. The app does not request your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, or advertising identifiers. It does not include advertising, analytics, crash-reporting, or social SDKs that send data to us.

We do not:

  • Create an account or require you to sign in
  • Send game progress, scores, or settings to a server
  • Use advertising IDs, cookies, or similar tracking for our own purposes
  • Sell, share, or rent personal information

Information stored on your device

To let you continue a game and remember preferences, Cocoa Boom stores data only on your device. That may include:

  • In-progress board, score, and best score
  • Settings such as language, sound effects, background music, and haptics
  • Theme preference

This data never leaves the device through our app. On Android, Auto Backup / cloud backup of app data is disabled for Cocoa Boom. On iOS, we do not use iCloud for game saves; local data may still be included in a full device backup that you control. Deleting the app (or clearing app storage on Android) removes local data. We cannot recover it for you.

Permissions

Release builds may use optional in-game haptics you can turn off in Settings (Android vibration permission where required; iOS Taptic Engine). The app does not request internet, storage, camera, microphone, contacts, or location access, and it does not use tracking (IDFA / App Tracking Transparency) for our own purposes.

Internet access

Gameplay, audio, and saves work without a network connection. The release app is not designed to contact our servers. On Android it does not declare the INTERNET permission. On iOS, App Transport Security remains in force; we do not require network access for play.

Children

Cocoa Boom is a puzzle game suitable for a general audience. It has no social features, ads, or in-app purchases in this version. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13 as defined by the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), because we collect no personal information at all.

Your privacy rights

Because we do not collect personal information, there is nothing for us to access, correct, export, or delete on our side. If you use the app in California or another jurisdiction with a consumer privacy law, we do not sell or share personal information as those laws define those terms.

You can remove local app data at any time by uninstalling Cocoa Boom, or on Android by clearing the app’s storage in system settings.

Third parties

If you download Cocoa Boom from Google Play or the Apple App Store, the store operator’s privacy policy applies to that download and to any information the store collects. We do not receive a copy of your Google or Apple Account details from gameplay.

Changes

If a future version adds networking, accounts, ads, in-app purchases, analytics, or online crash reporting, we will update this policy before that release. When this policy changes, we will update this page and the “Last updated” date. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the revised policy.
