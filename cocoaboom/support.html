Cocoa Boom Support

Chocello Games · Free, offline, no ads

Cocoa Boom is a chocolate-themed block puzzle for Android and iOS. Drag pieces onto the board, clear completed lines, and chase a new BEST score. It works without an internet connection, does not show ads, and does not include in-app purchases in this version.

Requirements

Android 7.0 (API 24) or later, or a compatible iPhone / iPad running a supported iOS version (see the App Store listing for the current minimum). No account is required.

How to play

Drag a piece from the tray onto the 8×8 board. When a full row or column is filled, those cells clear and you score points. Clear more at once for bigger rewards. When no piece can fit, the round ends—then try again to beat your BEST.

Start / Continue
Tap Start Game for a new run. If you leave mid-game, Continue Game restores the board and score stored on this device.
Settings
Open the gear in-game to toggle sound effects, background music, haptics, language, and theme. Changes are saved on the device.
Offline
Airplane mode is fine. All art, audio, and saves are local.

Common questions

Does the app need the internet?
No. Play, music, and saves stay on your device.
Is there an account, cloud save, or leaderboard?
No. Progress stays on this device. Uninstalling or clearing app storage deletes that data. We cannot restore it.
Why won’t Continue appear?
Continue only appears after you have started a round and there is a valid local save. A finished (deadlocked) round clears the continue save and keeps your BEST.
How do I turn music or vibration off?
Open Settings (gear) during a game and toggle Music, Sound, or Haptics.
The app feels stuck after I switch apps.
Bring Cocoa Boom back to the foreground. The round should resume; if music was on, it should return when you are back in play (and Settings is closed).
